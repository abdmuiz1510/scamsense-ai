About ScamSense AI

A Malaysia-focused hybrid scam detection and cyber awareness system.

🎯 What ScamSense AI Does

ScamSense AI empowers Malaysian citizens to quickly inspect suspicious messages claiming to represent Maybank, CIMB, LHDN, PDRM, or courier delivery services. It highlights psychological coercion triggers, identifies sensitive credential demands (TAC/OTP), and provides actionable safety steps.

💡 How the Hybrid Detection Engine Operates

The application pairs a deterministic heuristic rule engine tailored for Malaysian phrasing (in both English and Bahasa Malaysia) with optional Google Gemini AI contextual evaluation. The rule engine runs entirely client-side with zero latency and complete reliability, ensuring users are protected even without internet or API keys.

⚠️ Limitations & Official Educational Disclaimer

"ScamSense AI is an educational and awareness tool. Its results are based on detected indicators and automated analysis. It cannot guarantee that a message is genuine or fraudulent. Always verify suspicious communications through independently obtained official channels."
